I understand the importance of the mms_id, by the ExLibris Leganto Docs:

"MMS id. When included, will act as if to add repository citation and metadata 
fields from the repository record and will override any field in the citation.
When not included, will act as a non-repository citation. And in that case,
even if a match to an existing record is found, the citation metadata field
will not be overridden."

However, the POSTed Chapter details in the metadata, start and end pages, and
possibly more values are not directly copied over to the new Citations 
regardless of whether the mms_id is present or not.

Seeking some clarity on this.
